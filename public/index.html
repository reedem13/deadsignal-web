Co-op infiltration for up to 5 players · prototype

or join with a code
Phone: left thumb = move (push to edge = run, loud) · CROUCH = slow & quiet, hides behind crates · PRONE = crawl, very hard to spot · ACT = take down a guard from behind / press a door switch / fire your weapon if drawn · weapon button = draw / holster (you start with fists) · GEAR = items + weapons
Keyboard: WASD/arrows move · Shift run · C crouch · Z/X prone · E/Space act (takedown from behind, switch, else fire if drawn) · G draw/holster pistol · F fire (only when drawn)
Tunnels (yellow-striped mouths): crawl in to switch to first person — up/W forward, left/right turn. Guards can't follow.
Climbing (ACT button changes to CLIMB / MANTLE / GRAPPLE...): ladders + drainpipes (yellow) — ACT or keep pushing into them, push in = up, away = down, ACT = let go · crate lips (light trim) = MANTLE · orange rings = GRAPPLE, top-only: from the roof ring RAPPEL down, or PULL UP a teammate waiting at the foot ring (GRAB ROPE) · drop from high standing = loud thud (crouch first = quiet) · up high guards spot you less.
Co-op climb works on almost any building wall, tall crate or catwalk lip (gold ring shows the spot; the hazard-stripe 2P wall by the signal shed is the marked one): one player faces the wall, gun holstered, ACT = kneel (BOOST), a teammate ACT = climb on and get thrown up. Or whoever is on top stands at the lip and ACT = PULL UP the teammate below.
Gear: you start unarmed. GEAR button (top right) / I or Tab opens the equipment grid — pick a weapon to equip it, an item to use it (solo pauses). Find gear in lockers and crates, or search a guard you killed or took down (one drop each). South gate: P-9, stun grenades, a ration. Weapons: P-9 (+SUPP. = quiet), FR-5 rifle (bursts, loud), MK-T tranq (sleep), STUN.G (throw, stuns 5 m), C4 (plant, then DET / T). Items: RATION (+40 LIFE), C.BOX (hide: stand still), SCOPE (zoom), STIM (steady aim). Weapon box bottom-right = tap for the menu, item box bottom-left = tap to use (Q). 1-5 quick-swap once you own them (1 P-9, 2 FR-5, 3 MK-T, 4 STUN.G, 5 C4).
View: 1ST button (top right) / V = first-person on/off; tunnels switch to first person by themselves.
Team: host + up to 4 joiners share the same code. Door switches and extract still need just 2 agents; the rest are free agents.
