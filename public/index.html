Phone: left thumb = move (push to edge = run, loud) · CROUCH = slow & quiet, hides behind crates · PRONE = crawl, very hard to spot · ACT = take down a guard from behind / press a door switch / fire your weapon if drawn · weapon button = draw / holster (you start with fists) · GEAR = items + weapons
Keyboard: WASD/arrows move · Shift run · C crouch · Z/X prone · E/Space act (takedown from behind, switch, else fire if drawn) · G draw/holster pistol · F fire (only when drawn)
Water: you start swimming. Steady strokes are quiet. C / CROUCH is a short dive. Swim to a hull platform, then ladder, cargo net, low deck or 2P boost to board. Guards do not swim.
Bilge (yellow mouth on MERIDIAN's south hull): crawl in for first person — up/W forward, left/right turn. Guards can't follow. A ladder at the far end comes up on deck.
Climbing (ACT button changes to CLIMB / MANTLE / GRAPPLE...): ladders, cargo nets + drainpipes — ACT or keep pushing into them, push in = up, away = down, ACT = let go · crate lips = MANTLE · orange rings = GRAPPLE, top-only: from the roof ring RAPPEL down, or PULL UP a teammate waiting at the foot ring · drop from high standing = loud thud (crouch first = quiet).
Co-op climb works on bridge faces, gunwales and the marked 2P stripes on the hulls: one player faces the wall, gun holstered, ACT = kneel (BOOST), a teammate ACT = climb on. Or whoever is on top stands at the lip and ACT = PULL UP.
Gear: you start unarmed. GEAR / I or Tab opens the grid. CINDER's deck has the P-9 locker. Better kit is up ASH HOLD's crane and deckhouse, on MERIDIAN's bridge, or down in a cargo hold (C4). Search a guard you dropped for one bundle. Weapons: P-9 (+SUPP.), FR-5, MK-T, STUN.G, C4. Items: RATION, C.BOX, SCOPE, STIM.
View: 1ST button (top right) / V = first-person on/off; tunnels switch to first person by themselves.
Team: host + up to 4 joiners share the same code. Door switches and extract still need just 2 agents; the rest are free agents.
